Our Story

A Nkumba favorite, one plate at a time

Cafe Jambo started as a small neighborhood spot in Nkumba and grew into a place where students, families, and travellers passing through stop for a proper meal. We keep the menu honest — fresh fish, generous biryani, and coffee worth slowing down for.
